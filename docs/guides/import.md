# Bringing your blog over from Echo of Dreams

The import copies Echo of Dreams' blog here: every published post with its address, date, tags and category, its
pictures, and its authors. Nothing is announced or emailed while it runs. The rules behind it:
[specs/import.md](../specs/import.md).

## Before you start

- The blog needs a bucket for pictures (`Blog__Bucket__*`). Without one, pictures stay out and the log says so.
- Bylines on (Account → This blog), so readers see who wrote what.

## Run it

1. Open **Account**, then **Bring a blog over from Echo of Dreams**.
2. Leave the two addresses as they are, unless EoD's moved:
   - **Echo of Dreams' address**: `https://serverapi-staging.up.railway.app`
   - **Where profiles live**: `https://chat.echoofdreams.eu` (each author's link points to their profile there)
3. Tap **Import**. It runs in the background; the screen counts as it goes. A big blog takes a few minutes, mostly for
   the pictures.

When it's done: posts imported, pictures copied, pictures left out, new writers. **What happened** lists anything left
out and why: a picture only adults may see on EoD doesn't come over, and its description takes its place in the post.

**Run it again** whenever you like: posts already here are skipped, and only new ones come over.

## The authors

Each EoD author is now a writer here, under their own name, with their EoD profile as their link. Their posts say
"By Anna" from the start. They haven't signed in yet: on **People** they show as "Imported: hasn't signed in yet".

Tap **Invite to claim** beside them and send them the link. Signing in from it makes them that account, with all their
posts. The link works once, for 7 days, like any invitation.

## Old links

EoD's post addresses (`blog.echoofdreams.eu/a-post`) move to `/posts/a-post` by themselves. Author pages keep their
addresses. If a post's address was taken here, it moves aside, and the old address still leads to it.
