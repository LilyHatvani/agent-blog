# Editing a post on your phone

You can change a post's title, summary and text in the app, and fix an agent's proposal before you approve it. Every
save is a revision named after you, so you can always see who wrote what and put an older version back.

## Edit a post

1. Open the **Posts** tab and tap the post. Any post can be edited except one in the trash.
2. Under **Preview**, tap **Edit**.
3. Change what you need:
   - **Title**
   - **Summary** (for lists and link previews)
   - the body, in Markdown, on the **Write** tab
4. Tap **Preview** to see the text exactly as readers will get it. It is the same cleaning the public pages use.
5. Tap **Save**. It stays off until something changed, and only what you changed is sent. **Cancel** leaves the post
   as it was.

On a live post the editor says "This post is live: saving changes it for readers at once." There is no separate
publish step for a live post's edits.

## Approve with your edits

On an **Inbox** card where an agent wants to change, publish or schedule a post, you can fix it first.

1. Tap **Edit before approving**.
   - For a change, the editor opens on the agent's new version.
   - For a publish or schedule, it opens on the post as it is.
2. Make your changes. Use **Preview** to check them.
3. Tap the button at the bottom:
   - **Approve with my edits** for a change,
   - **Publish with my edits** for a publish,
   - **Schedule with my edits** for a schedule.

For a change, the agent's version is saved under the agent's name, and yours goes on top under your name, so each of
you keeps your words. For a publish or schedule, your fix is saved first and the post goes live, or is scheduled, with
it.

**Edit before approving** isn't offered for unpublishing, trashing or restoring (there's no text to change), or when
the post has changed since the agent asked.

## See and undo changes

The post's screen lists every revision under **History** as "Revision N by …". Tap one to read it, then
**Put this version back** to restore it. That makes a new revision with the old content; nothing in the history is
lost.

## If something goes wrong

- "The post changed while you were editing (an agent saved a new revision). Your text is still here: copy what you
  need, cancel, and edit the new version." Your text wasn't saved and nothing was overwritten. Copy it, tap **Cancel**,
  tap **Edit** again and paste it into the new version.
- "title is 1–300 characters." The title is empty or too long. **Save** is off while the title is empty.
- "The post changed since the agent asked, so this request no longer applies. Nothing was changed." On an Inbox card:
  someone saved the post after the agent asked. Nothing was written, your edits included. Decline it, or let the agent
  ask again.
- If an edit is refused for any other reason, nothing is written and the request keeps waiting, so you can try again.
